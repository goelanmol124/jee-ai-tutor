# Sources and evidence log

**Live-research cut-off:** 18 July 2026  
**Scope:** Indian privacy, cybersecurity, child protection, AI transparency, educational-AI safety, model-provider handling, and research ethics for the private tutoring pilot.

Only official, openly accessible, or lawfully mirrored public material was used. No Sci-Hub or paywall bypass was used. Legal conclusions in `synthesis.md` prioritize the enacted text and commencement notifications over explanatory notes, press coverage, or search summaries.

## Public concerns and app-review sources

These sources are deliberately separated from law and official guidance. They are useful for product-risk discovery, not prevalence estimates or verified factual findings.

### Public-discourse research

- *Group-Differentiated Discourse on Generative AI in High School Education: A Case Study of Reddit Communities* (March 2026), full open text: https://arxiv.org/html/2603.24972v1
  - Corpus: 3,789 posts from five education-related subreddits.
  - Reported comparison used: teacher `harms_learning` 55.2% versus student 39.7%; teacher `helps_learning` 32.2% versus student 12.8%; teachers also expressed more potential-to-help framing.
- *ChatGPT vs Teachers vs Students: Large-Scale Analysis of Generative AI Discourse in Education Communities on Reddit* (May 2026), full open text: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.17712v1
  - Themes used: hallucination/reliability, academic integrity, false-positive detection, grades/reputation, procedural fairness, teacher workload, and policy uncertainty.
- Open Reddit stakeholder dataset description (2022–2025): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2026.112871

## Research limitations

- A live-web search is not a substitute for an official Gazette, case-law, or paid legal-database update. Counsel should rerun the legal-status check immediately before recruitment and again before 13 May 2027.
- No later MeitY notification changing the DPDP child age, granting this operator an age exemption under section 9(5), or appointing Board members was found in targeted searches. This is an absence-of-evidence statement, not a legal representation that none exists.
- App reviews and Reddit posts are self-selected, mutable, sometimes anonymous, and vulnerable to fabrication. They were synthesized as design signals without identifying users.
- Provider pages describe general products. Only the signed DPA/order form and the selected endpoint/configuration can establish actual retention, training, review, location, and deletion behavior.
- The ICMR child guidance is directly aimed at biomedical research. A competent ethics body must decide the route for this educational intervention.

## Citation convention

Exact section/rule references in `synthesis.md` are controlling where page numbering differs between a PDF viewer, Gazette pagination, and extracted text. For DPDP materials, "Gazette p." refers to the printed English Gazette page. For international reports, page references refer to the report's printed page number.
