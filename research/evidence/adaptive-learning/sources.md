# Sources and evidence map

## Reading and citation method

- Ten lawful full-text papers were downloaded to `papers/` and reviewed end to end, including methods, results, discussion, limitations, references, and appendices where present.
- Page references below are **PDF file page numbers** (`PDF p.`), counted from the first page of the local file. This avoids ambiguity when an author manuscript’s file page differs from the journal’s printed page.
- `Full-text status: complete` means the complete local PDF was readable and included in the page-by-page review. None came from Sci-Hub or another pirate source.
- Citation keys `[P1]`–`[P10]` are used in `synthesis.md`.
- Social/forum materials `[A1]` onward are explicitly anecdotal. Direct Reddit endpoints returned 403 in this environment; Reddit patterns were reviewed through indexed public search excerpts and reporting that quotes/links the discussions. No Reddit anecdote is treated as causal evidence.

## Downloaded full-text research corpus

### [P1] Worked examples in mathematics

Barbieri, C. A., Miller-Cotto, D., Clerjuste, S. N., & Chawla, K. (2023). *A Meta-analysis of the Worked Examples Effect on Mathematics Performance*. Educational Psychology Review, 35, 11.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-023-09745-1
- Lawful full text: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9885171/
- Local PDF: `papers/barbieri-2023-worked-examples-meta.pdf`
- Full-text status: complete; publisher open-access/PMC copy; 33 PDF pages.
- Key support:
  - construct, cognitive-load rationale, and acquisition/practice distinctions: PDF pp. 3–8, Introduction;
  - methods, eligibility, and moderator limits: PDF pp. 8–18, Methods;
  - `g = 0.48`, 95% CI `[0.36, 0.60]`, `I² = 93.72%`: PDF p. 19, Overall Effects;
  - publication-bias analysis: PDF pp. 19–21;
  - moderator results: PDF pp. 21–23;
  - interpretation, prior-knowledge caveat, self-explanation result, measurement limitations: PDF pp. 23–28, Discussion/Conclusions.
- Use in synthesis: worked examples, cognitive-load reduction during acquisition, fading toward independent practice, and the warning that example variants are heterogeneous.

### [P2] Interleaving

Brunmair, M., & Richter, T. (2019). *Similarity Matters: A Meta-Analysis of Interleaved Learning and Its Moderators*. Psychological Bulletin, 145(11), 1029–1052.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000209
- Lawful full text: https://www.psychologie.uni-wuerzburg.de/fileadmin/06020400/2019/Brunmair_Richter_in_press__2019_META-ANALYSIS_OF_INTERLEAVED_LEARNING.pdf
- Local PDF: `papers/brunmair-richter-2019-interleaving-meta.pdf`
- Full-text status: complete; institutional author manuscript; 68 PDF pages.
- Key support:
  - overall `g = 0.42`; mathematics `g = 0.34`; words `g = -0.39`: PDF p. 2, Abstract;
  - theory, definitions, and mixed mathematics evidence: PDF pp. 4–16;
  - selection, coding, and moderator methods: PDF pp. 17–26;
  - similarity and material moderators: PDF pp. 27–31;
  - boundary conditions and publication-bias/robustness discussion: PDF pp. 31–39;
  - full effect-size table: PDF pp. 54–57, Table 2 and following tables.
- Use in synthesis: interleave confusable categories for discrimination; do not apply interleaving as a universal cadence.

### [P3] Online scaffolding

Doo, M. Y., Bonk, C. J., & Heo, H. (2020). *A Meta-Analysis of Scaffolding Effects in Online Learning in Higher Education*. International Review of Research in Open and Distributed Learning, 21(3), 60–80.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.19173/irrodl.v21i3.4638
- Lawful full text: https://www.irrodl.org/index.php/irrodl/article/view/4638
- ERIC record: https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1267049
- Local PDF: `papers/doo-2020-online-scaffolding-meta.pdf`
- Full-text status: complete; publisher open-access copy; 21 PDF pages.
- Key support:
  - scaffolding definition, ZPD framing, fading/transfer of responsibility: PDF pp. 3–5, Literature Review;
  - search, inclusion, and coding: PDF pp. 5–9, Method;
  - overall `g = 0.866`, 95% CI `[0.660, 1.072]`: PDF p. 10, Results;
  - moderator findings: PDF pp. 10–13;
  - publication-bias, higher-education scope, and limited fading evidence: PDF pp. 9, 13–15, Limitations/Future Directions.
- Use in synthesis: contingent assistance and eventual fading; effect-size estimate is not generalized directly to JEE secondary students.

### [P4] Mastery learning

Kulik, C.-L. C., Kulik, J. A., & Bangert-Drowns, R. L. (1990). *Effectiveness of Mastery Learning Programs: A Meta-Analysis*. Review of Educational Research, 60(2), 265–299.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.3102/00346543060002265
- Lawful full text: https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED299729.pdf
- Local PDF: `papers/kulik-1990-mastery-meta.pdf`
- Full-text status: complete; ERIC-hosted report/manuscript; 36 PDF pages.
- Key support:
  - intervention components and study coding: PDF pp. 2–7;
  - mean achievement effect `d = 0.52`: PDF p. 8, Examination Performance;
  - follow-up effect `d = 0.71` in 11 studies: PDF p. 14;
  - time-on-task and design moderators: PDF pp. 14–21;
  - completion/attrition effect `h = -0.14` for PSI: PDF pp. 22–23;
  - local versus standardized test discrepancy and interpretation: PDF pp. 24–29, Discussion.
- Use in synthesis: mastery packages can help but thresholds, corrective opportunities, time cost, and completion are inseparable.

### [P5] Spaced retrieval

Latimier, A., Peyre, H., & Ramus, F. (2021). *A Meta-Analytic Review of the Benefit of Spacing out Retrieval Practice Episodes on Retention*. Educational Psychology Review, 33, 959–987.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10648-020-09572-8
- Lawful full text: http://www.lscp.net/persons/ramus/docs/EPR20.pdf
- Local PDF: `papers/latimier-2021-spaced-retrieval-meta.pdf`
- Full-text status: complete; author-hosted manuscript; 29 PDF pages.
- Key support:
  - questions and headline results: PDF p. 1, Abstract;
  - distinction among spacing, testing, expanding, and uniform schedules: PDF pp. 2–7;
  - inclusion and robust-variance methods: PDF pp. 8–15;
  - spaced versus massed corrected `g = 0.74`, 95% CI `[0.55, 0.91]`: PDF p. 16 and pp. 20–21;
  - expanding versus uniform `g = 0.034`, 95% CI `[-0.10, 0.17]`: PDF p. 18 and pp. 21–23;
  - practical implications and limits: PDF pp. 23–25.
- Use in synthesis: prioritize spaced retrieval but do not claim expanding intervals are inherently superior.

### [P6] Knowledge tracing

Shen, S., Liu, Q., Huang, Z., Zheng, Y., Yin, M., Wang, M., & Chen, E. (2024). *A Survey of Knowledge Tracing: Models, Variants, and Applications*. IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies, 17, 1898–1919.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1109/TLT.2024.3383325
- Lawful full text: https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.15106
- Local PDF: `papers/shen-2024-knowledge-tracing-survey.pdf`
- Full-text status: complete; arXiv author preprint v4; 22 PDF pages.
- Key support:
  - taxonomy and statement that no single KT model is always best: PDF p. 3;
  - BKT parameters, guess/slip/learning/forgetting, and prerequisite-aware variants: PDF pp. 4–7;
  - individualization, engagement, and forgetting variants: PDF pp. 7–13;
  - recommendation/adaptive-learning applications and joint knowledge-level/structure need: PDF pp. 13–15;
  - datasets and real-world context: PDF pp. 15–16;
  - prediction-centric evaluation does not directly show practical effectiveness: PDF p. 17, Future Directions;
  - conclusions: PDF p. 18.
- Use in synthesis: maintain uncertainty and multiple evidence features; do not equate predictive accuracy with policy effectiveness.

### [P7] Retrieval-practice transfer

Pan, S. C., & Rickard, T. C. (2018). *Transfer of Test-Enhanced Learning: Meta-Analytic Review and Synthesis*. Psychological Bulletin, 144(7), 710–756.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000151
- Lawful full text: https://psycnet.apa.org/manuscript/2018-20773-001.pdf
- Author copy: https://sc-pan.github.io/pdf/PR_2018W.pdf
- Local PDF: `papers/pan-rickard-2018-retrieval-transfer-meta.pdf`
- Full-text status: complete; APA accepted manuscript; 130 PDF pages.
- Key support:
  - overall `d = 0.40`, 95% CI `[0.31, 0.50]`, and moderator summary: PDF pp. 3–4;
  - definitions of near/far transfer categories: PDF pp. 8–31;
  - search, coding, and effect-size methods: PDF pp. 31–45;
  - category and moderator results, including elaborated retrieval and initial success: PDF pp. 46–70;
  - limits, publication bias, and three-factor transfer framework: PDF pp. 70–76;
  - exact category estimates—application/inference `d = 0.33`; problem solving `d = 0.29`, nonsignificant: PDF pp. 121–130, Tables 2–8.
- Use in synthesis: require successful, aligned, elaborated retrieval and verify transfer on unseen problems.

### [P8] Prerequisite-graph sequencing

- Local PDF: `papers/tong-2019-cognitive-structure-adaptive-learning.pdf`
- Full-text status: complete; author-hosted conference paper; 9 PDF pages.
- Key support:

### [P9] BKT mastery threshold

- Local PDF: `papers/fancsali-2013-bkt-mastery-threshold.pdf`

These are not evidence of effect size or causality. They identify usability, motivation, implementation, and gaming risks to test in the AdvancedLearning pilot.

### Reddit access note

Search-indexed Reddit results on i-Ready/Khan/Anki exposed recurring complaints about repetition, progress loss, and review debt, but direct `reddit.com`, `old.reddit.com`, and Reddit JSON requests returned HTTP 403. To avoid laundering incomplete snippets into evidence, no individual Reddit claim is quoted as a major finding. The same candidate failure modes are preserved above through accessible public forums, YouTube transcripts, and reporting that directly quotes affected users. They should be validated with AdvancedLearning’s own interviews and telemetry.

## Traceability: major conclusions to local evidence

- Adaptive tutoring is positive but modest and context-sensitive: [P10], PDF pp. 2, 20, 28–31, 34–43.
- Mastery learning helps but creates time/completion trade-offs: [P4], PDF pp. 8, 14–23, 24–29.
- Spaced retrieval is robust; expanding schedules are not generally superior: [P5], PDF pp. 16, 18, 20–24.
- Interleaving should target discrimination and depends on similarity/material: [P2], PDF pp. 2, 24–39, 54–57.
- Worked examples are useful during acquisition but highly heterogeneous: [P1], PDF pp. 19, 21–28.
- Retrieval transfer exists but far/problem-solving transfer is conditional: [P7], PDF pp. 3–4, 46–76, 121–130.
- Scaffolding should be contingent and faded; the large pooled estimate has scope limits: [P3], PDF pp. 3–5, 9–15.
- Knowledge tracing must represent uncertainty and cannot be selected by prediction score alone: [P6], PDF pp. 3–7, 13–18.
- Graph-aware sequencing is promising but simulator evidence is not classroom proof: [P8], PDF pp. 1, 6–8.
- A mastery threshold encodes asymmetric false-positive/false-negative costs: [P9], PDF pp. 1–7.
